Controls:
WASD for movement
SHIFT to transform
ENTER to start
1 to perform vortex attack (available in boss form only)
MOUSE CURSOR to direct the bullets (available in boss form only)

How to play:
The goal is to defeat the purple boss while avoiding its bullets. However, the boss has a shield that prevents the player from killing it. So, the player needs to transform by gaining energy by flying close to enemy bullets. After transformation, the boss shrinks and takes full damage from the player. The player can finish the boss off by using the special vortex ability or directing bullets using the mouse, which creates a combo with damage multipliers up to 2 times.
